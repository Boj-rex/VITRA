This promotional collection is crafted directly from the aesthetic principles of high-conversion commercial advertising and fine-art tactile dimensional relief:

```
│   with color-blocked punch keywords    │   that stops the feed scroll  │

```

1. **Official Vitra Brand Color Palette:**
   - **Primary Accent:** Radiant Vitra Golden-Yellow (`#FECB0D` / `#F8D849`, directly extracted from the logo squircle).
   - **Primary Backdrop & Contrast:** Deep Vitra Forest Green (`#173B30` / `#0D261E`) and Obsidian Dark (`#09110E`) with luminous golden yellow rim lighting and ambient floor glow.
   - **Accents:** Emerald green (`#10B981`) verified tags and crisp white typography.
2. **Real Relatable Characters:** Dynamic, confident young Black Nigerian professionals reacting to interview invitations, celebrating job offers, and demonstrating the product.
3. **Dimensional 3D & Clay Objects:** Floating translucent glassmorphic notifications, 3D golden seals ("98% ATS"), exactly one floating 3D Paystack smartcard in the pricing showcase, and tactile rounded CTA pill buttons in Vitra gold.
4. **Artisan Papercraft Mastery:** A dedicated fine-art relief poster featuring intricate coiled ribbons, origami folds, and tactile seals in Vitra forest green and radiant gold.
5. **Official Brand Integration:** The official Vitra brand lockup (the signature yellow squircle icon + crisp white typography inside a frosted glass badge with golden border) is incorporated into every single design for strong brand recall and credibility.
